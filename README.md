 📊 HR Attrition & Employee Wellness Analysis

 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce patterns using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard was developed to explore employee demographics, attrition trends, job roles, employee satisfaction, workplace wellness, and other factors associated with employee turnover.

The goal of the analysis was to transform HR data into meaningful insights that could support employee retention, workforce planning, and data-driven HR decision-making.

 🛠️ Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Data Visualization
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Dashboard Development

 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis explored questions such as:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments and job roles have the highest attrition?
- How does attrition vary across employee demographics?
- How does employee satisfaction relate to attrition patterns?
- What workforce factors are associated with employee turnover?
- How do job level, business travel, work-life balance, and other employee factors differ across attrition groups?

 🔍 Key Insights

- The dataset contains 1,470 employees, of which 237 employees had exited, while 1,233 remained active.
- The overall employee attrition rate was approximately 16.1%.
- Attrition varied considerably across departments and job roles, with some roles contributing a larger share of total employee exits.
- Attrition patterns differed across employee age groups, job levels, and other demographic characteristics.
- The analysis examined the relationship between attrition and employee satisfaction, work-life balance, job involvement, and other workplace factors.
- Business travel and job-related factors also provided useful context for understanding employee turnover patterns.

 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, organizations could:

- Identify departments and job roles with consistently high attrition and investigate the underlying causes.
- Strengthen employee engagement and retention strategies for roles with higher turnover.
- Monitor employee satisfaction and work-life balance indicators as part of retention initiatives.
- Pay closer attention to early-career and lower-level employees who may require stronger onboarding, mentorship, and career-development support.
- Use HR analytics dashboards to continuously monitor attrition trends and support evidence-based workforce decisions.
